Spray Tan Pre-Care Advice

For the most flawless, even and long-lasting tan, please follow these simple steps before your appointment.

24–48 hours before your appointment

✨ Exfoliate
Give your skin a thorough exfoliation to remove dead skin cells and create a smooth base for your tan. Pay particular attention to dry areas such as elbows, knees, ankles and hands.

🪒 Shave or wax
If you need to shave, wax or use hair-removal products, try to do this at least 24 hours before your spray tan. This allows your skin time to settle and helps prevent irritation.

On the day of your appointment

🚿 Shower
Shower before your appointment if needed, but avoid applying moisturiser, body oils or other skincare products afterwards.

🚫 No lotions or oils
Please arrive with clean, dry skin. Avoid moisturisers, body oils, perfumes, deodorants, makeup and other products on the skin immediately before your tan, as these can create a barrier and affect how your tan develops.

👗 Wear loose, dark clothing
After your spray tan, wear loose-fitting, dark clothing and easy-to-slip-on footwear. Avoid tight clothing, socks and anything that may rub against the skin.

💍 Remove jewellery
Please remove jewellery before your appointment where possible.

A few extra tips

  • 🏃 Avoid intense exercise immediately before your appointment.
  • ♨️ Try not to book your spray tan immediately after a hot shower, sauna or steam room.
  • 🚫 Avoid applying deodorant or perfume directly before your appointment.
  • 💅 If you’re having a manicure or pedicure, ideally have it done before your spray tan, as soaking and exfoliating treatments afterwards can affect your tan.
  • 🧴 If you have particularly dry skin, moisturise regularly in the days leading up to your spray tan — just make sure your skin is clean and product-free when you arrive.

Most importantly…

Arrive with clean, dry skin and we’ll take care of the rest! 🤎

Spray Tan Aftercare Advice

Following these simple steps will help your tan develop evenly and fade naturally.

Before your first rinse

🚿 Wait for the recommended time
For a traditional spray tan, wait 8–12 hours before your first shower. If you have chosen a rapid or express tan, follow the rinse time advised during your appointment.

🚫 Keep your skin dry
Avoid sweating, swimming, rain, hot showers, saunas and steam rooms until after your first rinse, as moisture can cause streaking or uneven development.

👗 Wear loose, dark clothing
Continue wearing loose-fitting, dark clothing and avoid tight straps, socks or anything that may rub against your skin.

At your first rinse

💧 Use lukewarm water
Rinse gently with lukewarm water until the water runs clear. Avoid soap, shower gel, scrubbing or exfoliating during this first rinse.

🧴 Pat dry gently
Use a soft towel and pat your skin dry rather than rubbing it.

To help your tan last

  • 🧴 Moisturise daily with a gentle, hydrating moisturiser to help keep your skin smooth and your tan even.
  • ⚠️ Avoid oil-based products, as they can cause your tan to fade more quickly.
  • 🧽 Avoid exfoliating until you are ready for your tan to fade evenly.
  • 🛁 Keep showers lukewarm and avoid long, hot baths where possible.
  • ☀️ Remember that a spray tan does not provide sun protection. Continue to use a broad-spectrum SPF in the sun.

Enjoy your glow and contact us if you have any questions about your aftercare. 🤎
